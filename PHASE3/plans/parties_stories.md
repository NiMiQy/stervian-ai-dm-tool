# Party Narrative & Shared Story Synchronization Plan

## 1. Objective
Establish authoritative party narrative progression where only the party leader initiates actions for the entire group, non-leader members' action controls are locked, story turns are live-synced in real time via WebSockets, and narrative history is persisted to the database for all party members so each player retains complete story continuity upon leaving the party.

---

## 2. Core Mechanics & Rules

1. **Leader Authority (Single Actor)**:
   - Only the party leader (`is_leader = true`) can submit actions to the AI Dungeon Master while in a multi-player party.
   - The backend `/api/characters/:id/action` endpoint rejects non-leader actions with HTTP `403 Forbidden`.

2. **Member Action Lock**:
   - Non-leader party members have their action textarea disabled (`placeholder="Waiting for party leader ([Leader]) to take action..."`).
   - Submit button is locked with status badge `🔒 Party Member (Leader Decides)`.
   - Quick action choices dock is hidden for non-leaders.

3. **Live WebSocket Narrative Synchronization**:
   - When the leader's action resolves, the server broadcasts `party:story_turn` via WebSocket to all online party members.
   - Members' browser feeds immediately append the leader's action (`[Leader Quinn]: ...`) and the AI DM narration without reloading.

4. **Shared Database Persistence (Anti-Amnesia)**:
   - Each story turn generated by the leader is saved directly to the MySQL `stories` table for **every human party member** (`user_id`, `character_id`).
   - Prevents data loss and preserves full narrative history for all party members.

5. **Seamless Party Separation**:
   - When a member leaves the party (`leaveParty`), their action bar unlocks immediately.
   - Because all party turns were saved to their character's database history, the AI DM continues their solo adventure seamlessly from the exact narrative point of departure.
   - If the leader leaves, leadership transfers to the next player, whose action controls unlock automatically.

---

## 3. Data Flow

```text
[Leader Types Action]
        │
        ▼
POST /api/characters/:id/action
        │
        ├── Check Party Role: Must be Leader (rejects members with 403)
        ├── Build AI DM Prompt: Active companions formatted excluding leader
        ├── Call AI DM (DeepSeek V4): Generate story narration
        │
        ├── Persist to DB: Insert story turn for ALL party members (stories table)
        │
        ├── WebSocket Broadcast: Send party:story_turn to all member user_ids
        │       │
        │       └── Member Browsers: Append turn to feed & localStorage
        │
        └── HTTP 200: Return result to Leader Browser
```

## 4. Implementation Scope

### A. Backend
- **`src/services/aiPrompts.js`**:
  - `formatPartyContext(party, activeCharacter)`: Filter companions by `m.character_id !== activeCharacter?.id` (fixes companion exclusion bug).
  - Pass `character` into `buildDungeonMasterPrompt`.
- **`src/server.js`**:
  - In `POST /api/characters/:id/action`:
    - Enforce leader check via `getUserActiveParty(session.user_id)`.
    - Persist story turns for all human party members via `createStoryTurn`.
    - Broadcast `party:story_turn` via `broadcastToUsers`.

### B. Frontend
- **`public/js/containers/gameContainer.js`**:
  - `updateActionComposerState(partyData)`: Dynamically locks/unlocks input, button, and quick choices.
  - `onPartyEvent`: Listen for `party:story_turn` and append entries to feed and `conversationHistory`.
  - `act()`: Guard non-leader actions and reuse server-persisted `storyId`.
- **`public/style/party.css`**:
  - Styling for `.is-party-locked`, `.party-member-locked`, and `.party-lock-badge`.
